## 4. Source-fidelity decisions

- **XAG 108:** active gameplay and cinematics have separate pause atoms; the source saving/loading exception is retained. The reduced-control-scheme item is typed as `CONSIDER` rather than silently strengthened.
- **XAG 109:** tutorial availability, demonstrative tutorial form, revisitation, and voiced-tutorial subtitles are separate. A static controls/controller map alone is explicitly insufficient to satisfy the demonstrative tutorial atom.
- **XAG 110:** haptics can be disabled and strength-adjusted; when used to communicate information they cannot be the sole channel. Additional haptic cues are retained as a recommendation.
- **XAG 111:** pre-existing described broadcast media, localized descriptions for scripted media, and description timing are distinct. Natural gaps are preferred; extended description/pause is retained when gaps are insufficient.
- **XAG 112:** initial-accessibility navigation retains the source's strategy alternatives instead of requiring all of them simultaneously. Linear menu wrap is limited to one-axis menus; multidirectional menus remain an explicit exception.
- **XAG 113:** focus indication and the prohibition on invisible/off-screen focus are separate.
- **XAG 114:** hierarchy/context, focus shifts, purpose/operation alternatives, label association, assistive-tech parity, descriptive links/groups, help, form acceleration, headings, definitions, critical-text reading level, and settings preview remain distinct. Narrative/story text and proper names remain exceptions to the critical UI/settings reading-level atom.
- **XAG 115:** auto-detected errors retain the security/purpose exception. Destructive actions get review/confirmation/undo semantics, while a button hold cannot be the only confirmation method.
- **XAG 116:** core gameplay timing is explicitly out of scope. The four permitted time-limit adjustment mechanisms are encoded as an **alternative set with minimum one**, preserving the source instead of incorrectly requiring all four. The ≥10× adjustment, ≥20 second expiry-action window, and ≥10 extension-use semantics are retained.
- **XAG 117:** auto-updating and moving/blinking/scrolling controls preserve the ancillary-gameplay exception. Camera effects, repetitive motion, FOV, camera movement controls, and camera-view choice remain distinct; camera-view choice is typed as a recommendation.
- **XAG 118:** luminance flash, saturated-red flash, and spatial-pattern criteria remain conjunctive threshold bundles so definitions cannot be detached from their failure logic. The general test/avoid obligation is separate.
- **XAG 119:** incoming STT, outgoing TTS, text entry, incoming text narration, non-text communication narration, character-intent transcription, predefined-message preview narration, platform defaults, and in-game overrides remain distinct.
- **XAG 120:** communication accessibility is conditional on the title offering communication. Access/launch, prerequisite paths, settings, notifications, social/player actions, and chat operations are separately auditable.
- **XAG 121:** the WCAG 2 Level AA statement is preserved as Microsoft's documentation best-practice cross-reference only. This packet does not create legal or compliance certification.
- **XAG 122:** no-extra-cost support and multiple accessible support methods are distinct.
- **XAG 123:** harmful-content descriptions, launch warnings, contextual warnings, customization, skip controls, support/learning resources, accessible warnings/settings, and respectful representation are distinct. No live helpline identity is invented or asserted by this planning packet.
