## CHAR05:AGENCY-STATE-LATTICE

Character participation is represented by a non-scalar state lattice:

- VOLUNTARY — the actor currently elects to participate.
- CONDITIONAL — participation is offered only while explicit stated conditions remain satisfied.
- DEFERRED — participation is neither accepted nor refused; later reconsideration is permitted.
- REFUSED — the actor declines the specific request or route.
- WITHDRAWN — previously granted participation or delegation has been revoked.

Rules:
1. No state is automatically better, more loyal, more intimate, or more legitimate than another.
2. REFUSED and WITHDRAWN are legal outcomes and are not failure states to be ground away.
3. Gifts, repetition, prior success, relationship dimensions, public standing, legitimacy claims, or proximity cannot rewrite refusal as consent.
4. CONDITIONAL participation must expose its conditions rather than hiding them in a relationship score.
5. A route dependent on unavailable participation is invalidated locally and must recompute, substitute, defer, or fail legally.
6. Baseline shared play remains legal when a nonfoundational character route is refused or unavailable.

## CHAR05:RELATIONSHIP-HISTORY-LEDGER

Relationship history is append-only. Each relationship event is a typed record with:
- relative sequence relation;
- event class;
- public/private visibility class;
- optional evidence/provenance reference;
- per-dimension effect;
- unresolved or disputed interpretation where applicable;
- any agency transition caused by the event.

The six relationship dimensions remain independent:
- TRUST
- WARMTH
- RESPECT
- OBLIGATION
- RIVALRY
- CAUTION

A dimension effect may be INCREASE, DECREASE, UNCHANGED, MIXED_OR_CONTEXTUAL, or UNKNOWN. No aggregate total, average, reputation score, affinity scalar, or hidden weighted sum is permitted.

The ledger explicitly permits divergence, for example:
- TRUST increases while CAUTION also increases;
- RESPECT increases while WARMTH remains low;
- OBLIGATION decreases after repayment while TRUST is unchanged;
- RIVALRY persists while limited cooperation becomes possible.

Repair, retry, restitution, compensation, reconciliation, later success, or renewed contact may append new records but cannot erase breach, refusal, withdrawal, caution, or earlier material consequences.

## CHAR05:CHANGE-ARC-ALTERNATIVE-SET

The following are structural alternatives, not selected arcs or endings.

### ARC-A — reciprocity with retained boundary

A character may increase TRUST or RESPECT through reciprocal action while retaining explicit nonparticipation or disclosure boundaries. Care never creates capture, permanent obligation, or role occupancy.

### ARC-B — repair with residual caution

Restitution or repair may improve one or more dimensions while CAUTION remains elevated and the prior breach remains in history. Forgiveness, warmth, intimacy, legitimacy, or renewed delegation are not automatic.

### ARC-C — respectful distance

RESPECT may increase while WARMTH stays low and participation remains DEFERRED or REFUSED. Continued ordinary shared-space play remains legal without forcing closeness.

### ARC-D — bounded cooperation amid rivalry

RIVALRY may remain while a specific nonfoundational cooperative route becomes legal. Cooperation does not erase rivalry or establish representation, membership, friendship, romance, or family relation.

### ARC-E — delegation withdrawn, contact retained

A previously permitted mediation or delegated action may be WITHDRAWN while limited contact remains legal. Withdrawal does not rewrite earlier history and does not require hostility.

These alternatives can coexist as hypotheses. No arc is ranked, preferred, merged into a universal progression, or selected as canonical.

## CHAR05:PUBLIC-STANDING-SEPARATION

Institutional legitimacy and public standing remain separate from all six relationship dimensions.

Conversely, institutional recognition or public standing cannot force TRUST, WARMTH, RESPECT, OBLIGATION, RIVALRY, or CAUTION to any value.

No relationship event establishes ownership, office, membership, representation, jurisdiction, legitimacy, consent, or a final social role.
