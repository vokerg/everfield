### Candidate causal accounts

These are bounded **hypothesis classes**, not events or facts.

#### `WORLD03:ACCOUNT-MAINTENANCE-DIVERGENCE`

Question: could divergent maintenance or repair patterns contribute to a present condition?

Permitted support:
- repair/wear traces;
- operability changes with explicit relative ordering;
- material burden traces with responsibility unset.

Required uncertainty:
- who caused or omitted maintenance;
- whether the trace is causal rather than coincident;
- whether other pressures dominate.

Forbidden inference: a visible repair gap proves negligence, institutional fault, ownership failure, or a specific historical actor.

#### `WORLD03:ACCOUNT-RESOURCE-PRESSURE`

Question: could changing pressure on a shared physical dependency contribute to a present condition?

Permitted support:
- water-condition observations;
- ecological pressure indicators;
- practical-dependency traces;
- relative condition changes.

Required uncertainty:
- exact capacity and seasonal cycle;
- attribution of scarcity;
- entitlement or priority;
- exact timing.

Forbidden inference: physical scarcity or stress proves social entitlement, blame, deliberate restriction, or a schedule.

#### `WORLD03:ACCOUNT-ACCESS-USE-CONFLICT`

Question: could divergence between observed use and competing access/use claims contribute to a present condition?

Permitted support:
- scoped observed-use records;
- permitted-use claims;
- public physical access changes;
- maintenance-burden observations.

Required uncertainty:
- legal/social legitimacy;
- ownership/custodianship;
- whether conflict caused the physical condition or merely co-occurred with it.

Forbidden inference: frequency of use, prominence, or institutional assertion proves legitimacy or causal primacy.

#### `WORLD03:ACCOUNT-MULTICAUSAL-OR-UNDERDETERMINED`

Question: do the retained observations support more than one account, or fail to discriminate between accounts?

This account class is mandatory whenever available evidence cannot lawfully distinguish the alternatives above. It is not a “default truth”; it records the epistemic state `UNRESOLVED`.

- relative ordering does not itself prove causation;

- sibling social/character/narrative output cannot be used to close causal truth before fan-in.
