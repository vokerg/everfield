## 3. Institutional pressure model

The continuation models **pressures**, not new organizations. A pressure is a typed interaction among reviewed actors, obligations, scarce resources, claims, and branch-local consequences. It cannot create objective truth, private knowledge, or universal progression authority.

### `SOC-PRESS-001` — civic burden and legitimacy

Carriers: `FAC-COMMONS-01` and `COM-NEIGHBOR-01`.

Responsibilities include making public-work obligations legible, distributing burdens without silently converting volunteer/community participation into compulsory progression, and exposing a repair/alternative route when a material obligation is missed.

Incentives may conflict: coordination favors predictable contribution while community legitimacy can depend on fair burden distribution and visible alternatives. Neither public standing nor institutional endorsement proves a claim or grants secret access.

### `SOC-PRESS-002` — land/resource stewardship and scarcity

Primary carrier: `FAC-FIELDWARD-01`; counterpart interfaces use `WORLD_ROLE:COMMON_RESOURCE`, `WORLD_ROLE:CULTIVATION_ZONE`, and unresolved contested-site roles.

The pressure covers scarcity, stewardship, shared-stock allocation, land-use disagreement, and restoration burdens. Scarcity can limit specialist stock or coordinated support; it cannot silently deny baseline cultivation, ordinary acquisition, or unrelated foundational play.

A land-use assertion remains a claim until an external reviewed world fact binds it. Institutional confidence, repetition, or relationship state has no truth-promotion effect.

### `SOC-PRESS-003` — records, testimony, and confidentiality

Primary carrier: `FAC-ARCHIVE-01`, with provisional `CHAR_ROLE:*` testimony holders and `NARR_ROLE:*` disclosure contexts.

The Archive can classify records and apply confidentiality/purpose rules within reviewed authority, but record custody does not make a disputed account objectively true. Public records can change player exposure; they do not automatically update every character's knowledge. Private/secret access requires explicit holder disclosure or a separately validated authority effect.

### `SOC-PRESS-004` — repair capacity, access, and specialist coordination

Carriers: `FAC-MAKERS-01` and `FAC-WAYKEEPERS-01`.

The pressure covers constrained specialist capacity, repair/service obligations, safety constraints, and sponsored support. Capacity or safety denial must expose a typed reason and preserve a recovery, substitute, or ordinary-play alternative. Maker/waykeeper standing cannot become a universal repair, movement, or exploration tax.

## 5. Obligations and authority boundaries

`InstitutionalObligation` records separate duty, evidence, breach, repair, and authority scope.

A material obligation must identify:

1. obligor and beneficiary scope;
2. duty type and trigger;
3. admissible satisfaction evidence;
4. breach class;
5. repair/compromise/alternative routes;
6. branch applicability;
7. explicit authority exclusions.

The continuation uses four bounded obligation families: public work, stewardship/resource use, confidentiality/disclosure, and specialist service/safety. None may authorize coercive universal progression.

Authority remains domain-limited:

- civic coordination may coordinate public work but cannot create world truth or secret knowledge;
- stewardship may ration reviewed scarce specialist support but cannot own all baseline land/resource play;
- record custody may control bounded disclosure but cannot promote testimony to fact;
- specialist service/safety authority may constrain a bounded service but cannot close baseline movement/repair globally.

## 6. Claim, belief, testimony, knowledge, and exposure

The reviewed `SocialClaimBelief` fail-closed rule is retained. This continuation adds a `SocialDisclosureDecision` interface without changing truth authority.

A disclosure decision must bind a source/holder, recipient scope, information reference, authority path, exposure effect, and knowledge effect. Knowledge can change only when the record states a lawful disclosure/acquisition route. The following are explicitly insufficient on their own:

- high `public_standing`;
- high `trust`;
- faction membership or endorsement;
- player visibility;
- generated presentation;
- repeated testimony;
- institutional record status.

Disclosure never changes objective truth. It may create a durable history event and branch-local exposure consequence. Further sharing is not implied.

This directly preserves the reviewed vertical-slice boundary: a public record is not a secret, testimony can be unavailable without collapsing participation, and a deny-by-default secret stays unavailable unless an explicit disclosure/authority path succeeds.

## 7. Relationships, history, and anti-grind

Social current state remains multidimensional; institutional legitimacy is a separate multidimensional record. No universal score may combine them.

Material events include obligation fulfillment/breach, restitution, compromise, disclosure/withholding, exclusion, and alternative participation. Events are append-only in interpretation: a later repair can supersede a current penalty without deleting the breach or disclosure that motivated it.

Anti-grind constraints:

- repeated identical low-risk acts cannot raise all dimensions;
- standing cannot bypass confidentiality or evidence requirements;
- restitution changes only dimensions justified by typed cause/evidence;
- a material breach cannot be erased by unrelated repetition;
- access remains derived and scope-specific rather than a universal power level.
