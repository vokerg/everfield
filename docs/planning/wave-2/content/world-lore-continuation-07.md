## WORLD07:EVIDENCE-INDEPENDENCE-CLAIM-REGISTER

This interface deepens provenance-dependency handling by requiring every assertion of evidentiary independence to carry positive, scoped support. It prevents “no known link” from being laundered into “independent source.”

Required fields:

- `surface_ref`: exactly one member of the frozen three-member set;
- `source_evidence_refs`: immutable reviewed evidence references;
- `dependency_map_refs`: immutable reviewed provenance-dependency interfaces when available;
- `independence_claims`: zero or more pairwise or grouped claims;
- `shared_origin_claims`: zero or more supported common-origin claims;
- `unknown_dependency_refs`: unresolved provenance relationships;
- `scope_limits`: explicit scope of every claim;
- `counterevidence_refs`: retained contradictory provenance evidence;
- `private_sidecar_refs`: optional and deny-by-default;
- `authority_class: DEPENDENCY_CLASSIFICATION_ONLY_NOT_TRUTH`.

Allowed independence/dependency states are exactly:

- `SUPPORTED_INDEPENDENT_WITHIN_SCOPE`
- `SUPPORTED_SHARED_ORIGIN`
- `POSSIBLY_SHARED_ORIGIN`
- `DEPENDENCY_UNKNOWN`

Invariants:

- `SUPPORTED_INDEPENDENT_WITHIN_SCOPE` requires affirmative reviewed provenance evidence; absence of a dependency edge is insufficient.
- Shared-origin items cannot be multiplied into independent confirmations.
- Supported independence permits only separate provenance treatment; it does not establish credibility, confidence, truth, final causation, or authority.
- Unknown dependencies remain visible rather than silently normalized away.
- No numeric score, credibility scalar, confidence scalar, corroboration total, vote, threshold, rank, or preferred source exists.
- Institutional provenance, public standing, relationship state, repetition, unanimity, player exposure, or generated presentation cannot promote authority.

## WORLD07:OBSERVATION-SUFFICIENCY-MATRIX

This interface records whether the available reviewed observations are sufficient to perform a declared bounded comparison question. “Sufficient” means sufficient to execute that comparison without fabricating missing evidence; it never means sufficient to establish objective truth, final attribution, final site choice, or human quality.

Required fields:

- `surface_ref`;
- `comparison_question_id`: typed bounded question, never a final-canon question;
- `required_observation_roles`: qualitative observation roles needed by the declared comparison;
- `available_observation_refs`: immutable reviewed evidence references;
- `independence_register_ref`, when dependency analysis is relevant;
- `scope_coverage`: declared spatial/semantic scope only, never exact-time coverage;
- `counterevidence_refs`;
- `scoped_absence_refs`;
- `unknowns`;
- `sufficiency_state`;
- `authority_class: COMPARISON_EXECUTABILITY_ONLY`.

Allowed `sufficiency_state` values:

- `SUFFICIENT_FOR_DECLARED_BOUNDED_COMPARISON`
- `INSUFFICIENT_SCOPE`
- `DEPENDENCY_UNRESOLVED`
- `CONTRADICTORY_EVIDENCE_REQUIRES_RETENTION`
- `OBSERVATION_ROLE_UNFILLED`

Rules:

- No count of observations creates sufficiency by itself.
- A duplicated/shared-origin observation cannot fill two roles merely by repetition.
- A private-only observation cannot satisfy a required nonprivate role.
- A scoped absence is not converted into proof of nonexistence outside its declared scope.
- Contradictory evidence remains present and can keep closure unavailable.
- `SUFFICIENT_FOR_DECLARED_BOUNDED_COMPARISON` authorizes only the bounded comparison procedure. It does not select an account or world member and does not promote a claim into fact.
- Exact schedule, weather-window, travel-duration, timed-objective, or NPC-reachability sufficiency is outside authority and remains blocked.
